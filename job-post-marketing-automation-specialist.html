Marketing Automation Specialist

📍 Location: Remote (Brazil preferred) ⏰ Type: Full-time / Contract 📅 Posted: January 18, 2026

PearONE is growing! We're looking for a talented Marketing Automation Specialist to join our team and help us deliver exceptional CRM and email marketing solutions to B2B and B2C clients worldwide.

About PearONE

Based in Brazil and serving clients across the US, Canada, UK, and Latin America, PearONE is a senior-level marketing automation agency with 15+ years of experience. We work hands-on across strategy, execution, and optimization for enterprise CRM platforms including Salesforce Marketing Cloud, Microsoft Dynamics 365, HubSpot, and ClickDimensions.

What You'll Do

  • Build and manage email campaigns for B2B and B2C clients across multiple CRM platforms
  • Design marketing automation workflows including lead nurture journeys, drip campaigns, and customer lifecycle programs
  • Implement and troubleshoot CRM integrations, forms, landing pages, and tracking
  • Optimize deliverability through technical configuration, list hygiene, and compliance management (CAN-SPAM, GDPR, LGPD)
  • Collaborate directly with clients to understand requirements, provide recommendations, and deliver solutions independently
  • Create reports and dashboards to track campaign performance and provide actionable insights

What We're Looking For

Required Skills

  • 3+ years of hands-on experience with at least one major CRM platform (Salesforce Marketing Cloud, Dynamics 365, HubSpot, or ClickDimensions)
  • Strong technical foundation in email marketing including HTML, responsive design, and email testing
  • Experience building automation workflows including segmentation, triggers, and journey logic
  • Understanding of deliverability best practices and email compliance regulations
  • Excellent troubleshooting skills - you can diagnose and resolve technical issues independently
  • Fluent in English (written and spoken) for client communication
  • Self-directed and proactive - comfortable working remotely with minimal supervision

Nice to Have

  • Experience with multiple CRM platforms
  • Platform certifications (Salesforce, HubSpot, Microsoft)
  • SQL or AMPscript knowledge
  • Experience with B2B SaaS or enterprise clients
  • Portuguese fluency (bonus for Brazil-based candidates)
  • Experience with API integrations and data migrations

Why Join PearONE?

  • Work with top-tier clients across multiple industries and international markets
  • Diverse project portfolio - no two days are the same, constantly learning and growing
  • Fully remote - work from anywhere with flexible hours
  • Professional development - opportunities for platform certifications and training
  • Small team environment - your work directly impacts client success
  • Competitive compensation based on experience and performance

At PearONE, we believe in quality over quantity. We're a lean team that values deep expertise, independent problem-solving, and exceptional client service. You'll have the autonomy to make decisions, the trust to work independently, and the support of an experienced team when you need it.
